Product Overview

Built for demanding subsea data missions.

The SEALIEN AUV is a modular autonomous underwater vehicle platform designed for long-endurance subsea missions across offshore energy, marine research, hydrographic survey and environmental monitoring.

With configurable payload sections, precision navigation and mission-oriented autonomy, the platform enables efficient data acquisition in complex underwater environments.

Autonomy, payload flexibility and offshore reliability in one platform.

Autonomous Mission Execution

Modular Payload Architecture

System Architecture

Configurable by mission, serviceable by module.

Energy, control and navigation units are organized for upgrade paths, payload integration and field maintenance. The platform can be adapted for survey sensors, cameras, environmental packages and customer payload development.
